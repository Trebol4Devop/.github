<div align="center">
  <h3>ABOUT US</h3>
  <p>
    <b>Trebol4Devop</b> is a software engineering collective founded by developers from the <b>University of San Carlos of Guatemala (USAC)</b>.<br/>
    Inspired by the <b>four leaves of a clover</b>, we bring together four engineering disciplines in complete synergy: resilient architecture, DevOps automation, modern UI/UX design, and distributed persistence.
  </p>
</div>
